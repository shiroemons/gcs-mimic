Cloud Storage documents that "once a multipart upload is aborted, any parts in the process of
being uploaded fail" (https://docs.cloud.google.com/storage/docs/multipart-uploads). An abort no
longer waits up to catalog_wait_timeout_ms for a part whose body is still streaming. It answers
204 at once; the part fails with 404 NoSuchUpload when its commit finds the upload gone and
reclaims its staged bytes, and a part sent after the abort fails with NoSuchUpload. A completion
and an abort still wait for each other, and a completion still waits for the parts in flight.
With the abort lease forced back to waiting for the parts in flight, the registry test and
parts_of_different_numbers_and_uploads_do_not_exclude_each_other fail.
